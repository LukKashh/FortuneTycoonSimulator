IMPORT / DISPLAY GUIDANCE
-------------------------
1. These files are complete image sheets, not individual icon files.
2. Keep the supplied PNG files as the source/master assets. They have been
   included unchanged; no resizing, recolouring, cropping, or re-encoding
   has been applied.
3. For individual in-game graphics, crop each labelled card from its sheet.
   Use the visible card boundaries as the crop guides; do not include
   neighbouring cards.
4. The two sheets use different layouts. Do not assume a shared grid:
     - Business facilities: 2 columns x 5 rows.
     - Real-estate properties: 3 cards on the top row, 2 on the bottom.
5. Preserve proportions when scaling. Do not stretch assets to fit a
   different aspect ratio.
6. Preserve transparency where present. The property sheet is RGBA;
   retain its alpha channel. The business facilities sheet is RGB and
   includes its original dark checkerboard background.
7. These are presentation graphics for the in-game business / property
   selection and display UI. They are not tileable world textures.
8. Asset names are the labels visible in the supplied artwork. The two
   "Office Building" entries are distinct assets:
     - Business Facilities sheet: Office Building (business facility)
     - Real Estate sheet: Office Building (property)
