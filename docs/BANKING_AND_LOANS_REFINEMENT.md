# Capital Fortune — Banking & Loan Systems Refinement
## Google AI Studio project implementation specification

**Purpose:** Consolidate the approved Banking and Loan refinements for the existing Capital Fortune project. This document is a design and implementation specification, not permission to rewrite unrelated systems.

## 1. Scope and implementation principles

Refine the existing Banking and Loan systems while preserving the game's established identity, existing institutions, unrelated gameplay systems, and project structure.

- Inspect and reuse existing bank definitions, game-state models, financial actions, assets, and UI conventions wherever practical.
- Keep financial calculations and transaction authorization in the project's authoritative game layer. UI components must not independently authorize or fabricate balances, loans, interest, payments, or financial outcomes.
- Preserve existing player data and provide safe migration/default handling for new fields.
- Do not invent unapproved numeric rates, limits, probabilities, market schedules, loan amounts, fees, or formulas. Reuse valid existing configuration or make values configurable for later balancing.
- Do not redesign Tax functionality beyond giving it a separate tap-menu entry point.
- Before changing persistence architecture, inspect the active backend and use the existing architecture where possible. Do not introduce a second competing database as an incidental part of this refinement.

## 2. Tap-menu entry points

The tap menu must contain two distinct box options:

1. **Bank & Loans**
2. **Tax**

They open separate interfaces. Banking & Loans must not be embedded in Tax, and Tax must not be embedded in Banking & Loans.

## 3. Banking Access Menu and account limit

Selecting **Bank & Loans** opens a Banking Access Menu containing the available banking institutions as separate selectable listings. This is an institution-selection screen, not a generic account dashboard.

- Players can browse available banks whether or not they hold an account there.
- Selecting a bank opens that institution's own banking application experience.
- A player may maintain a maximum of **two active bank accounts globally**, across all institutions.
- Enforce this limit in authoritative game logic, not just by disabling a UI control.
- Account creation must be concurrency-safe so simultaneous requests cannot create a third active account.
- Browsing a bank must never create an account.

Closing an account releases its slot only after its financial obligations are resolved or transferred through a supported process. Any linked income sources must first be disconnected or reassigned without losing or duplicating income.

## 4. Separate simulated banking applications

Each institution must feel like its own digital banking application, not a generic screen with only its name or colour changed.

Each bank has its own:
- Branding and visual identity.
- Simulated login screen for existing customers.
- Simulated registration screen for new customers.
- Application shell, navigation, and dashboard.
- Institution-specific account information, services, actions, and activity.

Login and registration are **simulated in-game banking UX**. Do not introduce real-world banking authentication or external financial-account connections.

After simulated login, show that bank's dedicated application. Although the presentation is bank-specific, the underlying financial rules and authoritative financial records are shared and consistent.

## 5. Dynamic banking market

Bank benefits fluctuate **unpredictably**, not on a fixed weekly or monthly schedule. The four approved fluctuating categories are:

1. Loan interest rates.
2. Deposit interest rates.
3. Account maintenance fees.
4. Savings bonuses.

Rules:
- Each bank fluctuates independently.
- Conditions may improve or deteriorate.
- Changes are generated by the game economy, not triggered by player actions or merely by opening the app.
- A changed condition persists until a later market fluctuation.
- Current conditions must be visible in the relevant bank application and comparable from the access menu where appropriate.
- No bank should permanently dominate the market.
- New applications and future transactions use applicable current conditions.
- Existing loan agreements retain their agreed contractual interest terms; a market change must not silently rewrite an active loan.

Do not invent fixed fluctuation timing, probability, magnitude, or formula. Use existing project configuration where appropriate or make these settings configurable.

## 6. Bank Account Overview

Each bank's post-login dashboard must use conventional, polished, mobile-friendly banking-app UX. The delegated overview design should communicate at least:
- Current account balance.
- Account identity and status.
- Current applicable bank benefits and conditions.
- Linked businesses and real estate, where supported.
- Recent account activity.
- Relevant banking and loan actions.

Do not add new economic mechanics merely to fill dashboard space. Minor layout and presentation details are delegated to implementation.

## 7. Business and Real Estate income integrations

Business and Real Estate income linking are **features offered by a bank**, not separate bank account types.

A bank may independently support:
- **Business income linking:** link player-owned businesses so their generated income is deposited directly into the player's account at that bank.
- **Real Estate income linking:** link player-owned properties so their generated property income is deposited directly into the player's account at that bank.

Not every bank must support both integrations. A bank may offer one without the other.

### Income routing rules

Use these approved defaults:
- Multiple businesses may link to one bank account.
- Multiple properties may link to one bank account.
- Each individual business or property has only one active income destination at a time.
- Different assets may route income to different active accounts, subject to bank support and the global two-account limit.
- Players may change an asset's linked destination.
- A destination change applies to future income only; previously credited income remains in its original account.
- Closing an account requires linked sources to be disconnected or reassigned.
- Reassignment must be safely processed so income is neither lost nor duplicated.

Make it clear which bank account receives each asset's income. Linking an asset must not create an extra bank account. Business/property ownership remains separate from banking.

## 8. Loan products and lending categories

Banks are the primary lending institutions in this refinement. The initial loan categories are:

1. **Personal Loans** — general personal financing.
2. **Business Loans** — business operations, expansion, and investment financing.
3. **Real Estate Loans** — property acquisition, development, and property investment financing.

Banks may differentiate products through availability, interest rates and fees, maximum amounts, eligibility requirements, repayment conditions, and collateral requirements.

**Independent lending institutions are deferred to a later expansion.** Do not implement them as part of this scope.

## 9. Unified financial profile and credit assessment

All banks must use one consolidated player financial profile. Assessment considers:
- Total assets and liabilities.
- All outstanding loans across every institution.
- Existing repayment obligations.
- Relevant business and real-estate income.
- Available and already-encumbered collateral.
- Repayment history and financial standing.
- The applying bank's criteria and current product offer.

### Anti-stacking invariant

Borrowing capacity is shared across all banks and lenders represented in the game. A bank must not calculate a fresh independent credit allowance as if debt at other institutions does not exist.

Every application must:
- Include all existing debt in consolidated liabilities.
- Calculate remaining borrowing capacity after existing exposure and obligations.
- Apply the institution's own eligibility and product conditions.
- Prevent concurrent approvals from exceeding shared capacity.
- Avoid counting the same asset repeatedly as unencumbered collateral.

Possible assessment outcomes: **Approved**, **Approved with adjusted terms**, **Additional collateral required**, or **Rejected**.

Exact scoring weights, ratios, formulas, and thresholds are not yet specified. Keep them configurable; do not invent fixed design values.

## 10. Interest and repayment lifecycle

Interest must accrue consistently based on elapsed game time, independently of app-open frequency or client tick frequency. Track principal and accrued interest separately.

Support:
- Contractual interest rate and calculation terms.
- Scheduled repayments.
- Automatic payments from a designated bank account where supported by the agreement.
- Manual repayments.
- Partial principal repayment.
- Early repayment and full settlement.
- Outstanding balance tracking and repayment history.
- Missed-payment detection and overdue status.
- Contractual late consequences and default processing.

New applications use current market conditions. Existing loans retain agreed terms unless the player enters an explicitly approved renegotiation or refinancing agreement.

Interest accrual, repayment processing, and loan-state transitions must be authoritative, idempotent, and safe against duplicate processing.

## 11. Collateral and secured lending

Potential collateral categories:
- Real estate properties.
- Businesses.
- Other valuable assets where supported by the existing catalogue and lending model.

Rules:
- The player must own the asset.
- One asset cannot secure multiple active loans simultaneously.
- Collateral is registered against its specific loan.
- Banks may apply their own collateral requirements.
- Pledged assets remain identifiable in the player's portfolio and loan details.
- Selling or transferring pledged assets requires loan settlement or an approved collateral-release process.
- A fully settled secured loan releases its collateral.
- Changes in asset valuation may affect financial standing and future assessments.

In default recovery, collateral may be recovered according to the loan agreement and game rules. Any remaining debt continues to count toward consolidated liabilities. Recovery percentages and sale formulas are not specified; do not invent them.

## 12. Delinquency, default, recovery, and rehabilitation

Implement an actual delinquency lifecycle, not merely unused status fields. Cover:
- Missed scheduled-payment detection.
- Overdue loan status.
- Contractual late fees or penalties where configured.
- Credit-standing consequences.
- Default declaration according to configured agreement conditions.
- Collateral recovery for secured loans where applicable.
- Continued tracking and recovery of any remaining debt.
- Financial rehabilitation through subsequent responsible repayment.

Consequences must be proportionate to the agreement and independent of app-open frequency or client tick frequency. Grace periods, penalty amounts, default thresholds, and rehabilitation formulas remain configurable until specified.

## 13. Early repayment, closure, and refinancing

Support:
- Partial repayment.
- Early repayment.
- Full settlement.
- Correct settlement of accrued interest.
- Collateral release after settlement.
- Loan closure.
- Refinancing through a new application and fresh credit assessment.
- Historical records of repaid, closed, and refinanced loans.

Refinancing must not erase previous loan or repayment history. Record it as a distinct financial event and preserve accurate consolidated debt throughout the transaction.

## 14. Loan dashboard and cross-bank visibility

Each bank's application has its own loan area, bank-specific products, and agreements. The player's consolidated financial position must nevertheless remain consistent and visible across both bank relationships.

The loan overview should communicate:
- Active loans and lending institutions.
- Outstanding principal and accrued interest.
- Upcoming repayment obligations.
- Repayment schedules and status.
- Early repayment actions.
- Pledged collateral.
- Historical loan records.
- Consolidated debt across banking relationships.

A bank-specific interface must not hide debt at other institutions from credit assessment or imply lower total liabilities.

## 15. Technical integrity

All financial rules must execute in the authoritative game logic and persistence layer. The client is a presentation and input layer only.

Use atomic or equivalent concurrency-safe processing for:
- Opening and closing bank accounts.
- Enforcing the two-active-account limit.
- Linking and changing income destinations.
- Loan application and approval.
- Reserving collateral.
- Accruing interest.
- Processing scheduled and manual repayments.
- Refinancing and releasing collateral.
- Default and recovery transitions.

Use idempotency protection for scheduled processing and financial actions. Never trust client-supplied balances, timestamps, loan eligibility, collateral ownership, interest amounts, or payment outcomes.

Inspect the project's actual active backend before implementation. Do not assume documentation accurately describes runtime architecture. Do not silently replace the existing stack or introduce a second competing persistence system. If the current architecture cannot support authoritative financial processing, document the gap and propose the smallest coherent correction.

## 16. Required regression tests

At minimum, test:
1. A player cannot open a third active bank account.
2. Concurrent account-opening requests cannot exceed the two-account limit.
3. Browsing a bank does not create an account.
4. Each bank opens its own simulated login/registration and application UI.
5. Market fluctuations do not rewrite existing loan agreements.
6. A business or property cannot have two simultaneous income destinations.
7. Multiple businesses and properties can route income to one supported account.
8. Different assets can route to either of the player's two active accounts.
9. Destination changes affect future income only.
10. Account closure is blocked until linked sources are disconnected or reassigned.
11. A loan at one bank reduces available borrowing capacity at another.
12. Concurrent loan approvals cannot exceed consolidated borrowing capacity.
13. The same collateral cannot secure two active loans.
14. Interest accrual is consistent regardless of login or tick frequency.
15. Scheduled repayments cannot be processed twice.
16. Partial and full repayment update principal, interest, balance, history, and collateral correctly.
17. Overdue and default transitions follow configured contractual conditions.
18. Refinancing preserves history and never temporarily erases consolidated liabilities.
19. Client attempts to fabricate balances, payments, or loan approvals are rejected.
20. Bank-specific loan views and consolidated reporting agree on total obligations.

## 17. Implementation sequence

Implement incrementally, preserving existing functionality and verifying each phase before proceeding:

1. Banking tap-menu entry, institution access menu, and two-account enforcement.
2. Simulated bank login/registration and bank-specific application shells.
3. Shared account records and account overview.
4. Dynamic banking-market benefits.
5. Business and Real Estate income linking and routing.
6. Unified financial profile and consolidated credit assessment.
7. Loan products and bank-specific application/approval.
8. Interest accrual and repayment lifecycle.
9. Collateral registration and release.
10. Delinquency, default, recovery, and rehabilitation.
11. Early repayment, closure, refinancing, and history.
12. Cross-bank reporting, regression tests, and mobile UI verification.

Do not combine all phases into one unreviewable rewrite.

## 18. Explicitly deferred or unspecified

Do not silently decide:
- Independent lending institutions (deferred).
- Exact market fluctuation timing, probability, and magnitude.
- Exact rates, fees, limits, repayment terms, and eligibility formulas.
- Exact credit-scoring weights and thresholds.
- Exact overdue grace periods and default thresholds.
- Exact collateral recovery valuation and sale formulas.
- Additional bank products or mechanics not listed here.

Use existing project configuration where appropriate, keep unapproved balancing values configurable, and request a design decision only when implementation cannot safely proceed without one.

**End of specification.**
